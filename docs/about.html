About Cullie's Lawn Care

Cullie's Lawn Care is a locally owned and operated lawn-care business serving the Spartanburg, SC area. With a focus on cool-season turf, professional granular fertilizer programs, and commercial-grade mowing, we deliver reliable, high-quality results for every yard we touch.

From weed control to fungicide applications, aeration, overseeding, and soil improvement, we take pride in offering services that keep your lawn healthy, green, and thriving all year long.

Whether you need routine maintenance or specialized treatments, we bring precision, consistency, and genuine care to every job. Our commitment is to build lasting relationships with our customers by delivering exceptional results, every single time.

Why Choose Us?

  • Local Expertise: Deep understanding of Spartanburg's climate and soil conditions
  • Professional Equipment: Commercial-grade tools and products for superior results
  • Personalized Service: Custom treatment plans tailored to your lawn's specific needs
  • Reliable Results: Consistent, high-quality work you can count on
  • Customer Care: Transparent communication and responsive support
