Our Services

We offer a comprehensive range of professional lawn care services tailored to keep your Spartanburg lawn healthy, beautiful, and thriving all year long.

Lawn Mowing & Maintenance

Clean, consistent cuts with commercial-grade equipment, precise edging, and thorough blowing for a polished finish every visit.

Granular Fertilizer Programs

Seasonal applications specifically tailored for cool-season turf to enhance color, density, and overall lawn health.

Weed Control

Selective, professional treatments targeting broadleaf weeds, crabgrass, and invasive species without harming desired turf.

Fungicide Applications

Professional-grade treatments for fairy ring, brown patch, dollar spot, and other lawn diseases common to Spartanburg.

Aeration & Overseeding

Core aeration to relieve soil compaction, followed by premium seed blends for thicker, healthier turf coverage.

Soil Boosters

Biostimulants, soil conditioners, and organic amendments to improve root health and soil structure long-term.
