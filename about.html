About Cullie's Lawn Care

Cullie's Lawn Care is a locally owned and operated lawn-care business serving the Spartanburg, SC area. With a focus on cool-season turf, professional granular fertilizer programs, and commercial-grade mowing, we deliver reliable, high-quality results for every yard we touch.

From weed control to fungicide applications, aeration, overseeding, and soil improvement, we take pride in offering services that keep your lawn healthy, green, and thriving all year long.

Whether you need routine maintenance or specialized treatments, we bring precision, consistency, and genuine care to every job.
